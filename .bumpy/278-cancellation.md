Breaking: `GenericAbortSignal` now extends `AbortableSignal`, so it requires `addEventListener` and `removeEventListener` typed `(type: "abort", listener: () => void) => unknown` (TypeScript rejects one that can't take that call, such as one with a third required parameter), and a `signal` without them (such as `{ aborted: false }`) rejects with an `ERR_BAD_OPTION_VALUE` `FaxiosError` before middleware runs, instead of a raw `TypeError` or being silently ignored when `timeout` is set. To migrate, pass a real `AbortSignal` (from an `AbortController` or `AbortSignal.timeout()`), or wrap a polyfilled signal in an object that implements `addEventListener` and `removeEventListener`. A request with a long-lived caller `signal` and a `timeout` (and each `retry` try with `attemptTimeout`) no longer stays linked to that signal after it settles, so reusing one signal for many requests no longer grows memory. A `responseType: "stream"` body behind `retry({ attemptTimeout })` now stays under the request's `timeout` and `signal` until it is read or cancelled, so a stalled body rejects with `ETIMEDOUT` instead of hanging.
