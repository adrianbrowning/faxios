Breaking: `resolveRequestURL()` and `getUri()` throw a `FaxiosError` with `ERR_INVALID_URL` for a config with neither `url` nor `baseURL`, where they returned `undefined`, so `resolveRequestURL()` always returns the string its type promises; a `URL` url now resolves as its `href` with `params` added, instead of throwing a bare `TypeError` or coming back as the `URL` object. `post`, `put`, `patch`, `query`, `postForm`, `putForm` and `patchForm` now send headers given as a `Headers`, a `Map` or `[name, value]` pairs, which they dropped, and `create({ headers })` with one of those keeps the inherited `common` and method groups and `defaults.headers.common`. A `responseType: "stream"` body stays under the request's `timeout` and signal when middleware passes `next` a context with a new `ctx.state`. A request that sends the XSRF header, or a custom `authBearer` header, refuses redirects when `fetchOptions.redirect` is an own `undefined`, as it does when the key is missing. The `transitional`, `retry`, `authBearer` and `forwardTo` option fields take `undefined` under `exactOptionalPropertyTypes`. `FaxiosLike` is exported from the package root again, marked `@internal`. Importing faxios outside a browser no longer parses the page origin URL.
